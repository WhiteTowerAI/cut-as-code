# Motion graphics recipe attribution

HyperFrames conversions and adapters in this repository are local modifications. They retain
upstream credit comments where present, but a converted copy remains subject to the license and
attribution terms of its actual source.

See motion-anything's attribution at that revision:
[ATTRIBUTION.md](https://github.com/nexu-io/motion-anything/blob/b016900d9ee92fc2d3e4dc520359cc8999d2ed4e/ATTRIBUTION.md).

notices. Its attribution states that 35 recipes in `recipes/web/` are ports of React Bits effects
and that source components are preserved in `recipes/imported/`.

## `codrops/`

The Codrops collection contains 36 projects selected from the
[Codrops demos catalog](https://tympanus.net/codrops/demos/) plus the Kinetic Images project
described below. Each project is bound to its own source repository and immutable commit.
The source-of-truth catalog is
[`codrops/SOURCE_CATALOG.json`](codrops/SOURCE_CATALOG.json), which records every project URL,
commit, license evidence, and local directory. Its license evidence distinguishes projects with a
local MIT license file from projects covered by the official Codrops licensing statement.

The collection includes **Kinetic Images** by Dominik Fojcik / Codrops, sourced from
[DGFX/codrops-kinetic-images](https://github.com/DGFX/codrops-kinetic-images/tree/965dda362a8f9e5d522ed675493897200d273e49)
at revision `965dda362a8f9e5d522ed675493897200d273e49`. See
[`codrops/KineticImages/LICENSE.codrops`](codrops/KineticImages/LICENSE.codrops). Codrops states
that its downloadable demos are MIT-licensed unless otherwise stated; see the official
[Codrops licensing page](https://tympanus.net/codrops/licensing/).
